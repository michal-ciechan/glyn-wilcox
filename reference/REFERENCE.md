# Glyn Wilcox: reference pack

Source material for designing and building the website samples. All facts below come from the verified research run of 2026-11-17. Items marked ⚠ come only from Glyn's own bio, so present them as his story and not as independently verified fact.

## Who he is (one line)

Glyn Wilcox is a ska and reggae singer, vinyl DJ and radio presenter from Torbay, South Devon. His brand is **Ska Au Go Go**.

## Facts

### Radio: Palm Torbay
- He presents **"The Ultimate Ska & Reggae Show"** (the artwork also titles it "The Ska and Reggae Show").
- It airs **Sundays 7–9pm** on **Palm Torbay**, a volunteer-run local station on the English Riviera. Station strapline: "Reinventing Local Radio" / "Your Number 1 Hit Music Radio Station".
- The music covers ska, reggae, rocksteady, dub and vintage sounds, in a two-hour show.
- He also appears on the Wednesday night edition of **"The Love Zone"**.
- Listen live: https://player.aiir.com/palmtorbay/ (station site https://www.palmtorbay.com)
- Show archive on Mixcloud (station account): https://www.mixcloud.com/JayofTheBay/glyn-wilcox-ska-au-go-go-palm-torbay-show-24/ ("Show 24", 1h55m). Use it as an embed example.
- His Palm Torbay bio describes him as "DJ, Singer, Songwriter".

### Band: The Simmertones
- He is the **lead vocalist** of The Simmertones, a **nine-piece ska band from Devon/Torbay**, active since about 2007. He sings and also toasts.
- Discography (from Last.fm):
  - *Presenting The Simmertones, Volume 1* (EP, 2009, produced with Richard Digby Smith)
  - *Everything's Comin' Up Ska* (EP, 2011)
  - *Something for the Weekend?* (album, 2013)
  - *Ten Feet Tall* (album, 2019)
- Press line from Last.fm: "the magical vocals of Glyn Wilcox".
- Festivals the band has played: Bestival, Camp Bestival, Looe Music Festival, London International Ska Festival, Respect Festival (Plymouth), Kingsbridge Music Fest.
- They supported **Madness** at the House of Fun Weekender in 2018.
- Their *Doctor Who* theme cover got plays on BBC Radio 1, Radio 2 and 6 Music.

#### Band extras (research run of 2026-11-24, for the band-site concepts)
- *Presenting The Simmertones, Vol. 1*: a "seven track mini-album featuring three original tracks and four cover versions" (Punktastic review, March 2010). Originals: "One Time Soon", "Warm Your Bones" and "Do What You Got To Do" (the closing track). The covers include "Sammy Dead", the *Doctor Who* theme, and reworks of Justin Hinds and The Skatalites. The full track order is not confirmed.
- *Everything's Comin' Up (Ska)* EP (2011, self-released): four tracks: "Bring Your Love To Me" (original), "Blam Blam Fever" (The Valentines cover), "King of Ska" (Desmond Dekker cover) and "A Little Time" (original). Review quote: "The vocals are good, the horns are perfect…" (ReadJunk, 1 Oct 2011).

- *Ten Feet Tall* (2019): the tracklist is not confirmed.
- Single: "Perhaps Perhaps Perhaps" (Last.fm, 2:28). The year is not confirmed.

### Career story ⚠ (his own bio)
- He has collected reggae records since he was 10.
- He has spent about 25 years in ska and reggae bands, including **Too Hot** and The Simmertones.
- He has supported The Skatalites, Desmond Dekker, Laurel Aitken, The Pioneers, The Beat, The Selecter and Madness.
- Festivals: Bestival, Boomtown, Looe, Love Summer. Airplay: BBC Radio 1, 2 and 6 Music.

**Short bio (~50 words):**
Glyn Wilcox is a Torbay-based singer, vinyl DJ and radio presenter. He fronts nine-piece Devon ska band The Simmertones, hosts *The Ultimate Ska & Reggae Show* every Sunday on Palm Torbay, and spins original ska, reggae and rocksteady on 45s at his Ska Au Go Go nights across South Devon.

**Long bio (~150 words):**
Glyn bought his first reggae records at ten and never stopped. Over twenty-five years he has sung and toasted with ska and reggae bands, first Too Hot and then The Simmertones, the nine-piece Devon outfit behind *Something for the Weekend?* (2013) and *Ten Feet Tall* (2019). Along the way he has shared stages with the people who wrote the rulebook: The Skatalites, Desmond Dekker, Laurel Aitken, The Pioneers, The Beat, The Selecter and Madness. There have been festival slots from Bestival to Looe, and plays on BBC Radio 1, 2 and 6 Music.
These days you'll also find him behind the decks. On Sunday nights he is on Palm Torbay with two hours of ska, rocksteady, reggae and dub. Out and about he runs Ska Au Go Go, a vinyl-only night of boss sounds where the dress code is sharp and the dancefloor never sits still.
