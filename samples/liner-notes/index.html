Twenty-five years on the offbeat.

Glyn Wilcox is a Torbay-based singer, vinyl DJ and radio presenter. He fronts nine-piece Devon ska band The Simmertones, hosts The Ultimate Ska & Reggae Show every Sunday on Palm Torbay, and spins original ska, reggae and rocksteady on 45s at his Ska Au Go Go nights across South Devon.

Ska Au Go Go: vinyl nights

Out and about, Glyn runs Ska Au Go Go, a vinyl-only night of boss sounds where the dress code is sharp and the dancefloor never sits still.

Nothing is played from a laptop. The records are original ska, reggae and rocksteady pressings on seven-inch 45s, drawn from a collection he started at the age of ten.4 He also plays live sets of ska and reggae covers.

The story so far

Glyn bought his first reggae records at ten and never stopped.4 Over twenty-five years he has sung and toasted with ska and reggae bands, first Too Hot and then The Simmertones, the nine-piece Devon outfit behind Something for the Weekend? (2013) and Ten Feet Tall (2019).

Along the way he has shared stages with the people who wrote the rulebook: The Skatalites, Desmond Dekker, Laurel Aitken, The Pioneers, The Beat, The Selecter and Madness. There have been festival slots from Bestival to Looe, and plays on BBC Radio 1, 2 and 6 Music.4

These days you’ll also find him behind the decks. On Sunday nights he is on Palm Torbay with two hours of ska, rocksteady, reggae and dub. Out and about he runs Ska Au Go Go, a vinyl-only night of boss sounds where the dress code is sharp and the dancefloor never sits still.
