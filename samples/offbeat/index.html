Twenty-five years on the offbeat

Glyn bought his first reggae records at ten and never stopped.

Over twenty-five years he has sung and toasted with ska and reggae bands, first Too Hot and then The Simmertones, the nine-piece Devon outfit behind Something for the Weekend? (2013) and Ten Feet Tall (2019). There have been festival slots from Bestival to Looe, and plays on BBC Radio 2 and 6 Music.

These days you'll also find him behind the decks. On Sunday nights he is on Palm Torbay with two hours of ska, rocksteady, reggae and dub. Out and about he runs Ska Au Go Go, a vinyl-only night of boss sounds.
