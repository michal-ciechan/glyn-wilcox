Ska Au Go Go: vinyl nights

Away from the radio, Glyn takes the records out. Ska Au Go Go is a vinyl-only night of boss sounds: original ska, reggae and rocksteady on 45s, played across South Devon as DJ Glyn Wilcox.

Twenty-five years on the offbeat

Glyn bought his first reggae records at ten and never stopped. Over twenty-five years he has sung and toasted with ska and reggae bands, first Too Hot and then The Simmertones, the nine-piece Devon outfit behind Something for the Weekend? (2013) and Ten Feet Tall (2019).

Along the way he has shared stages with the people who wrote the rulebook: The Skatalites, Desmond Dekker, Laurel Aitken, The Pioneers, The Beat, The Selecter and Madness. There have been festival slots from Bestival to Looe, and plays on BBC Radio 1, 2 and 6 Music.

These days you'll also find him behind the decks. On Sunday nights he is on Palm Torbay with two hours of ska, rocksteady, reggae and dub. Out and about he runs Ska Au Go Go, a vinyl-only night of boss sounds where the dress code is sharp and the dancefloor never sits still.

Career details are Glyn's story as told in his own Palm Torbay bio, where he is listed as DJ, singer and songwriter.
