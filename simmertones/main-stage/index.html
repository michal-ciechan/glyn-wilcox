Bio length

The Simmertones are a nine-piece authentic ska band from Torbay, South Devon. Fronted by singer and toaster Glyn Wilcox, they mix their own songs with classic ska and rocksteady covers. They have played Bestival and Looe Music Festival, supported Madness in 2018, and had their cover of the Doctor Who theme played on BBC Radio 1, 2 and 6 Music.

The Simmertones are a nine-piece ska band from Torbay, South Devon, and have been playing since about 2007. Glyn Wilcox sings lead and toasts up front, with a horn section and a rhythm section behind him. Their sets mix original songs with classics from the Jamaican songbook, from Desmond Dekker’s “King of Ska” to The Valentines’ “Blam Blam Fever”.

They have made four records: Presenting The Simmertones, Volume 1 (2009), the Everything’s Comin’ Up Ska EP (2011), the album Something for the Weekend? (2013) and Ten Feet Tall (2019). Their cover of the Doctor Who theme was played on BBC Radio 1, Radio 2 and 6 Music.

Festival slots include Bestival, Camp Bestival, Looe Music Festival, the London International Ska Festival, Respect Festival in Plymouth and Kingsbridge Music Fest, and in 2018 they supported Madness at the House of Fun Weekender.
