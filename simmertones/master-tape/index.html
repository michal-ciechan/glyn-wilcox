The band

Session notesSheet 1 of 1

The Simmertones are a nine-piece ska band from Torbay in South Devon, and they've been playing since about 2007. Their sets mix original songs with classic ska, rocksteady and reggae covers. The horns sit up front, and Glyn Wilcox sings and toasts.

Their cover of the Doctor Who theme was played on BBC Radio 1, Radio 2 and 6 Music. They've played Bestival, Camp Bestival, Looe Music Festival, the London International Ska Festival, Respect Festival in Plymouth and Kingsbridge Music Fest, and in 2018 they supported Madness at the House of Fun Weekender.

There are four records so far, from the 2009 mini-album Presenting The Simmertones, Volume 1 to Ten Feet Tall in 2019.
