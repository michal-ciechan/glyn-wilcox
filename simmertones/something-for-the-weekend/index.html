Short back and sides. Long on horns.

The Simmertones are a nine-piece ska band from Torbay, South Devon, and they have been playing together since about 2007.

The set mixes their own songs with classics from the ska and rocksteady songbook, played the old way with a horn section out front. Their cover of the Doctor Who theme was played on BBC Radio 1, Radio 2 and 6 Music, and in 2018 they supported Madness at the House of Fun Weekender.

Glyn Wilcox sings lead and toasts. By his own account he has spent twenty-five years in ska and reggae bands, sharing stages with The Skatalites, Desmond Dekker, Laurel Aitken, The Pioneers, The Beat, The Selecter and Madness.
