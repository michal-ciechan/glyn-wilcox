The band

The Simmertones are a nine-piece authentic ska and reggae band from Torbay in South Devon, gigging since about 2007. They play their own songs next to classic ska and rocksteady covers, with a full horn section and Glyn Wilcox singing lead and toasting.

They put their records out on their own label, Windrush Recordings, and they've played everywhere from Boomtown to Madness's House of Fun Weekender.
