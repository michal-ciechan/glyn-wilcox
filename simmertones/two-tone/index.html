Authentic ska, played the old way.

The Simmertones are nine musicians from Torbay in South Devon, and they have been playing together since about 2007.

They write their own songs and play the classics too: ska, rocksteady and reggae, with a four-piece horn section out front. Glyn Wilcox sings lead and toasts.

The band puts its records out on its own label, Windrush Recordings. The latest album is Ten Feet Tall (2019).
