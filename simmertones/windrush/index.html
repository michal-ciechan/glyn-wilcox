The band

The Simmertones are a nine-piece authentic ska and reggae band from Torbay in South Devon, gigging since about 2007.

They write their own songs and play the ska and rocksteady classics the old way, with a full horn section up front and Glyn Wilcox singing lead and toasting.

Their records come out on their own imprint, Windrush Recordings.

As last published, about 2016–17. To be confirmed with the band.
